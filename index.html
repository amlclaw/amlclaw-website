Still Screening Addresses One at a Time?

In 2026, most crypto compliance still runs on black-box scores, manual copy-paste, and hope.

🔒

"Trust Our Score"

The vendor gives you a number. You ask why. "Proprietary algorithm." Try explaining that to a regulator during an audit — or proving which funds were actually risky.

🐌

Manual, Address by Address

Paste one address, wait for the trace, copy-paste the report, repeat for the next fifty. Batch screening shouldn't require a spreadsheet and a Friday night.

📴

No Continuous Monitoring

You screen an address today. It gets frozen tomorrow. Nobody knows — because nothing re-checks it, and nothing re-checks its counterparties.

🙏

Compliance Debt

One-off screens don't build an audit trail. Regulators want evidence chains, history, and a defensible verdict — not a screenshot of a score.

Five Modules. One Compliance Desk.

From a single address to a portfolio of transactions — the whole pipeline runs on the width.info V3 engine.

01

KYA Address Screening

Screen any Ethereum or Tron address with multi-hop fund tracing (0–5 hops each direction). Server-side rulesets flag Sanctions, Cybercrime, Gambling, Freezing Actions — with per-rule path evidence and an interactive fund-flow graph.

→
02

KYT Transaction Screening

Screen a transaction's source of funds (in), destination (out), or both. Chainalysis-aligned alerts with DIRECT/INDIRECT exposure, per-direction rulesets (KYT-IN / KYT-OUT), and full path evidence.

→
03

Batch Screening

Paste up to 50 addresses or transaction hashes. Async mode with live progress, per-item risk + fund score, one-click full reports, and CSV export for your records.

→
04

Address Monitoring

Watch a watched address's future transfers — every new stablecoin transaction is KYT-screened on your schedule, with a full ledger and high-risk webhook alerts.

→
05

TX Monitoring

Watch a transaction's from/to counterparty. Periodic KYA re-screening tracks the risk trend and alerts the moment an address gets tagged Sanctions or Freeze, or its level escalates.
